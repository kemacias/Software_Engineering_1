## Imagine you're about to start a fixed-deadline project. How might you use WBS in concert with CPM in scheduling?

I would use the WBS to break down the project into smaller, manageable tasks, and then use CPM to schedule those tasks. This creates a good picture to see if the work fits into the deadline. 

## Does the measure of the team's velocity become more or less accurate over time?

More accurate. Random swings average out with more data points instead of swinging the whole thing. You see the trends and the outliers. 

## Even with velocity measurements, there's always some inaccuracy. If you were building a schedule, how would you deal with this inaccuracy?

Build a buffer in everywhere I can and give estimates instead of hard deadlines.
