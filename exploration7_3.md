## Kanban limits the number of tasks in each column to control the work flow. How does Scrum control it?
Scrum caps work per sprint instead of per column. The team works on only to the backlog items that they think they can finish in that time-boxed iteration. It is based on velocity, and nothing new gets added until the next sprint.

## Contrast Scrum's sprints with Kanban's continuous development. What are some of the benefits and drawbacks of each?
Sprints have predictable rhythm with regular check-ins, while Kanban allows for more flexibility.

## What are some arguments for more granular columns on the Board? What are some arguments for fewer columns?
They add more clarity to the work flow and reveal bottlenecks. But they also add more complexity and overhead.
