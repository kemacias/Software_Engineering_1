What are the differences between:

Constraints - Requirements that are dictated by regulations like laws and things like the FCC
Assumptions - Things you assume about the requirements that can change in the future
Non-functional requirements - Problems that you do not need to solve with the application
Invariants - Things that MUST be true no matter what
What's the Single Responsibility Principle in one small phrase? - A function should do just one thing really well

List as many benefits as you can think of that you get from adhering to SRP.

- Your code is testable by you and everyone after you
- If a function breaks, it doesn't break 5 different things because it was responsible for 5 different things
- The next guy working on your code isn't going to come after you for not following SRP
