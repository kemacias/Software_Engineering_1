## Contrast story points and T-shirt sizes as methods of estimating relative task size. Benefits and drawbacks?

Story points are actualy numbers that can be used to estimate time on a project. T-shirt sizes are easy for everyone to understand and demonstrate some of the scope of the project, but they aren't a unit of measure that can really translate to how much time you need.
