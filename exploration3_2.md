## 1. Why aren't we generally concerned with making the wireframes look aesthetically-pleasing?
### Wireframes are for the technical details of the user interface. They aren't intended to be something presented to stakeholders that aren't developers. Its a technical document for developers. 

## 2. Under what circumstances would you want mockups that show the screens in pixel-perfect detail?
### Mockups are best after the technical workflow of the product is worked out. This is also something that could be presented to a stakeholder that they would understand. These include details like color schemes and fine details of the user interface.
